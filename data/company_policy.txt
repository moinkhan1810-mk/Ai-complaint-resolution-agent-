COMPANY COMPLAINT RESOLUTION POLICY:

1. Shipping Delays:
   - If an order is delayed by more than 7 days, the customer is eligible for a 15% refund on the shipping cost.
   - If an order is delayed by more than 14 days, the customer is eligible for a full refund of the shipping cost and a $10 store credit.
   - Support agents must apologize for the delay and the lack of previous communication.

2. Escalation Rules:
   - If the customer has contacted support twice or more without a response, the issue MUST be escalated to a Tier 2 Support Manager.
   - Do not offer refunds for escalated Tier 2 issues directly; let the manager handle it.

3. Tone and Professionalism:
   - Replies must be empathetic, professional, and take ownership of the mistake.
